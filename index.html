A few checks before you donate

Donor Eligibility Checklist

🎂

Age

18–65 years

⚖️

Weight

Usually at least 45–50 kg, depending on local guidelines

🩺

Health

Normal blood pressure and pulse; hemoglobin typically above 12.5 g/dL

📅

Donation Interval

At least 3 months since your last whole-blood donation

These are general guideposts, not a medical assessment. Eligibility varies by location and individual health; the blood bank team will make the final determination.
