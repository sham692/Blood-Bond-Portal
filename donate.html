Record a blood donation. Every record is added to the blood group wise collection table in the admin panel.

Donation Details

A standard donation is 350 ml or 450 ml (450 ml = 1 unit).

Eligibility Checklist

  • Age between 18 and 65 years.
  • Body weight above 50 kg.
  • Haemoglobin level at least 12.5 g/dL.
  • At least 3 months gap since the last donation.
  • No fever or infection in the last 2 weeks.
